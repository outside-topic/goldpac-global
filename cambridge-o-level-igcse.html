Who This Programme is For

Cambridge O Level and IGCSE qualifications establish the academic bedrock for A Levels and university admission. Our cohorts are carefully grouped by student learning mode:

School Students Evening or weekend batches that reinforce difficult school topics (such as non-calculator algebra or chemical calculations).
Home-Schoolers Full primary syllabus instruction, laboratory practical theory, weekly homework marking, and termly parent reports.
Private Candidates Step-by-step guidance to register with local British Council centres and sit papers as an independent learner.
Grade Retakers Targeted mark recovery focusing on error logs and high-yield examination components.

Course Formats & Timetables

Standard Route

Two-Year Comprehensive

Covers the entire Cambridge syllabus systematically across 4 terms. Ideal for students starting in Year 9 or 10.

  • 2–3 live sessions/subject per week
  • 75 minutes per session
  • Systematic topical testing
Accelerated Route

One-Year Fast Track

An accelerated programme for mature students, private candidates, or students looking to sit exams a year early.

  • 4 live sessions/subject per week
  • Intensive homework feedback
  • Rapid mock exam progression
