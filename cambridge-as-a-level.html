Staged Assessment vs Linear Routes

Cambridge International AS & A Level offers flexibility in how candidates sit their components. GOALPAc prepares students under both recognized pathways:

Option A: Staged Assessment (Recommended)

Take the Cambridge International AS Level in Year 12 (carrying 50% of the total A Level marks), then complete the A2 Level components in Year 13.

Reduces examination stress into manageable stages.
Option B: Linear Assessment

Take all AS and A2 components together in the same examination series at the end of Year 13.

Common for fast-track independent students.

University Recognition & Competitive Entry

Competitive degree courses routinely require top grades with specific subject pairings:

Engineering & Physics

Maths (9709) + Physics (9702) + Further Maths (9231). Typical target: A* A* A.

Economics & Finance

Maths (9709) + Economics (9708) + Further Maths. Typical target: A* A A.
