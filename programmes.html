[ PROGRAMME 01 ]
Cambridge Assessment International Education

Cambridge O Level & IGCSE

Build the knowledge, skills and exam confidence you need for top grades in Cambridge O Level and Cambridge IGCSE. Our live online classes cover every syllabus topic in order, with regular past-paper practice so you learn exactly how each paper is marked.

  • Available Formats: 2-Year Standard Programme or 1-Year Fast Track.
  • Key Series: May/June and October/November.
  • Core Disciplines: Maths (4024), Physics (5054), Chemistry (5070), Biology (5090), Economics (2281), Computer Science (2210).

[ PROGRAMME 02 ]
Pearson Edexcel Awarding Body

Pearson Edexcel International GCSE

Graded on the internationally benchmarked 9–1 scale. Our Edexcel faculty focus intensely on paper structure, practical skill assessments (Paper 1 and Paper 2), and precise examiner mark allocations.

  • Linear 9–1 Grading: Targeted preparation for Grade 8 and Grade 9 (equivalent to high A*).
  • Series Availability: May/June series plus January series for selected subjects.
  • Top Subjects: Maths A (4MA1), Physics (4PH1), Chemistry (4CH1), Biology (4BI1), Business (4BS1).

[ PROGRAMME 03 ]
Cambridge Assessment International Education

Cambridge International AS & A Level

We provide rigorous training in complex analytical derivations, multi-step proofs, and examiner report pitfalls.

  • Staged vs Linear Routes: Sit AS in Year 12 and A2 in Year 13, or all papers linearly in Year 13.
  • Component Mark Schemes: Weekly past-paper marking matching CAIE Grade Thresholds.
  • Core Disciplines: Pure Maths & Mechanics/Stats (9709), Physics (9702), Chemistry (9701), Biology (9700), Economics (9708).

[ PROGRAMME 04 ]
Pearson Edexcel Modular IAL

Pearson Edexcel International A Level (IAL)

Modular flexibility: students sit unit exams across January, June, and October windows. If a particular unit mark needs improvement, students can retake that individual module without resitting the entire qualification.

  • Flexible Unit Sittings: Unit 1 to Unit 6 mapped per subject.
  • Three Exam Windows: Sittings in January, May/June, and October.
  • Disciplines: Pure Mathematics (P1–P4, M1, S1), Physics Units 1–6, Chemistry Units 1–6.
Enquire for Edexcel IAL Cohort
[ PROGRAMME 05 / HIGH VELOCITY ]

Crash Courses & Revision Bootcamps

An intensive 8-week sprint starting prior to every official Cambridge and Edexcel examination series. Designed for students who have covered syllabus topics in school or through self-study, but require high-impact exam technique, mark-scheme drills, and full timed mock examinations.

Topical Paper Sprints Focused revision of the 20 most frequently examined topic areas per subject.
2 Full Timed Mocks Conducted under strict unpaused exam-hall timers with annotated debriefs.
Examiner Error Logs Direct analysis of Cambridge/Edexcel Examiner Reports highlighting common traps.

1:1 Elite Private Tutoring
