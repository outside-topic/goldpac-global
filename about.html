Who We Are

We are a fully online academy teaching the Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel O Level, IGCSE and AS & A Level syllabuses to students around the world.

Our classes are live, small (capped strictly at twelve students) and focused on what examiners actually reward. Every lesson is recorded in high definition, every student is assigned an individual academic mentor, and every parent receives regular, honest written progress reports.

What GOALPAc Provides:

Live online courses for O Level & IGCSE
AS & A Level full syllabus preparation
Crash courses before every major exam series
1:1 Elite bespoke private tutoring
Step-by-step guidance for private candidates
Structured support for retakers & home-schoolers

[ 04 / METHOD ]

The GOALPAc Grade Plan: Five Steps to Your Target Grade

Every enrolled student follows our original five-step learning model designed to turn consistent effort into examiner marks.

1. Diagnose — 45-Minute Baseline Assessment

A comprehensive placement test plus academic interview identifies the student's current working level, foundational topic gaps, and exam prerequisites. Parents receive a detailed diagnostic report before classes commence.

2. Plan — Personal Study Roadmap

An individualized syllabus schedule maps weekly topics to the student's target examination series (May/June, Oct/Nov, or January). Incorporates target grade milestones and weak-area allocation.

3. Teach — Live Interactive Classes

2 to 4 live sessions per subject each week. Master teachers cover every topic in the syllabus specification with digital whiteboard notes, step-by-step proofs, and live student question-and-answer.

4. Practise — Weekly Quizzes & Timed Papers

Weekly topical quizzes reinforce core memory. Monthly timed papers are completed under exam-condition timers and marked strictly against official board mark schemes with line-by-line annotations.

5. Perfect — Revision Sprints & Full Mocks

High-velocity revision sprints prior to exam series. Two full unpaused mock exams with examiner-style feedback and an official Final Readiness Report before sitting the real board exam.

[ 05 / FACULTY ]

Academic Faculty & Teacher Standards

Our teachers are not generalist tutors. They are career educators with verified experience in preparing candidates for Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel examinations.

Faculty Recruitment Benchmark:

  • Relevant Honours Degree: Subject qualification in the discipline taught from an accredited university.
  • Minimum 3 Years Board Experience: Verifiable track record delivering O Level, IGCSE, or A Level results.
  • Demonstration Lesson Audit: Assessed by our academic panel on pacing, digital engagement, and mark-scheme precision.
  • Background & Safeguarding Verification: Criminal records check, reference audits, and identity validation.
[ 06 / SAFEGUARDING ]

Academic Quality & Student Safeguarding

Online education requires the highest standards of student safety and data integrity. GOALPAc enforces strict protocols to ensure our learning environment is safe, respectful, and quality-assured.

Zero Private Channels

All student-teacher communication takes place strictly within our official, monitored academy platform. No private unrecorded messaging is permitted.

Designated Safeguarding Lead

A named Designated Safeguarding Lead oversees all child-protection policies and responds promptly to any parental or student concern.
