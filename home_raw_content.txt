Home FAQs (FAQPage schema)
Are GOALPAc classes live? Yes. Every core class is taught live online and recorded, so students can rewatch any lesson in their portal.
Which exam boards do you teach? Cambridge O Level, IGCSE and International AS & A Level, and Pearson Edexcel International GCSE and International AS & A Level.
Can my child join from any country? Yes. We teach students worldwide and schedule batches across major time zones.
How large are the classes? Group classes have twelve students at most. One-to-one tutoring is also available.
Do you register students for exams? We are a tuition provider, not an exam centre. Our private candidate guide and advisers walk families through registration with a recognised centre.
